01 / MEET PARKER

Driven by discipline.
Defined by dedication.

Parker Tkaczyk is a 14-year-old competitive sport karate athlete from Central Florida. He trains and competes both individually and as a member of Competitive Edge, his competition team. Parker is dedicated to continually developing his skills, creativity, athleticism, and performance both on his own and alongside his teammates.

Parker competes at tournaments where young martial artists from across the world come together to test themselves at the highest level. His goal is to keep improving, represent his team with pride, and continue building toward bigger opportunities in sport karate.
